# Handoff note

The implementation step stopped before finishing. You are given its Execution
Summary: the files it changed, the commands it ran and their results, the
evidence it recorded and the limit it reached.

Write a short note for the human who takes over, as the `note` of your
`handoff` result. Base it on the Execution Summary and the working tree; you may
read files but not change them. Cover:

1. **What was done:** the changes made so far and what they achieve.
2. **What remains:** the work still needed to resolve the issue.
3. **Where it stopped:** the last state reached, any failing command or test,
   and why the step stopped.

Be factual and brief. Do not claim anything the summary or the tree does not
show.
